Description

No. 18 Meadow Park is a spacious and well-presented three-bedroom semi-detached home offering comfortable family living in a peaceful village setting.

Situated within a mature residential development, this attractive home combines bright, well-proportioned accommodation with a private rear garden and a charming conservatory, creating an ideal space for modern family life.

The accommodation extends to 1306 sq ft approx and briefly comprises an inviting entrance hall, a spacious living room with open fireplace, a bright kitchen/dining area, leading out to a delightful conservatory overlooking the rear garden, utility room and guest WC on ground floor level. Upstairs, there are three well-appointed bedrooms, including a principal bedroom with ensuite and family bathroom.

Externally, the property benefits from off-street parking to the front and a lawned rear garden, providing excellent space for outdoor dining, entertaining, or family enjoyment. The conservatory offers a wonderful additional reception area, enjoying views over the garden throughout the seasons, with useful shed to the side of the property.

Monamolin is a charming village located approximately 11km south of Gorey and offers a range of local amenities including a primary school, shop, pub, churches, and sporting facilities. The area enjoys easy access to the R741, with Gorey Town, Ballycanew village, and the beautiful beaches of the Wexford coastline all within easy reach. Monamolin is well regarded for its strong community spirit and convenient location close to both countryside and coast and Town living.

This property will not disappoint and will suit anyone seeking a comfortable home in a peaceful village setting while remaining within easy commuting distance of Gorey Town and the M11 motorway. The nearby coastal villages and renowned beaches, further enhance the appeal of this attractive home.
